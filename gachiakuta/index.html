GACHIAKUTA

STORY & ART BY KEI URANA (裏那圭)

A young boy named Rudo lives in the slums of a floating sphere city where society casts its trash down into the Abyss. Falsely accused of murder, Rudo is thrown into the dreaded Abyss—a desolate wasteland filled with terrifying trash beasts. Seeking vengeance and the truth behind his frame-up, Rudo awakens a power within himself to fight back.
